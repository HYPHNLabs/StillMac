# StillMac brand assets

The StillMac mark combines a continuous `S` observation path with three sample nodes. It represents recurring process observations without implying cleanup, intervention, speed, or causal diagnosis.

## Shipped files

- `stillmac-app-icon-matte-1024.png`: 1024 × 1024 matte app icon.
- `stillmac-logo-matte-dark.png`: 2172 × 724 matte horizontal lockup used in the README.
- `manifest.json`: dimensions, colour mode, provenance date, and SHA-256 checksums.

## Usage

- Keep the complete icon silhouette and outer margin intact.
- Do not recolour individual nodes or redraw the path.
- Do not place the icon over a visually busy image.
- Do not add an Apple logo, speedometer, broom, shield, or AI sparkle.
- Use the horizontal lockup only on dark backgrounds.

## Provenance and rights

Generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 12 August 2026 from a StillMac-specific creative brief. The brief requested an original macOS utility identity and explicitly excluded Apple marks and generic cleaner or AI imagery.
