# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally, critique ideas and code rather than people, respect privacy boundaries, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Expected behaviour

- Be specific, evidence-led, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while holding safety and quality boundaries firmly.
- Keep vulnerability details and private user data out of public discussions.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope, especially for destructive or privacy-sensitive features.
- Do not submit generated noise, fabricated test evidence, spam, or unsupported product claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualised conduct, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing secrets, telemetry, backdoors, destructive behaviour, or misleading claims.
- Repeatedly bypassing contribution, security-reporting, or safety requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour threatens people, privacy, security, or the project. Reports should be made privately through the repository owner's available private channel.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of reporters or affected participants.
